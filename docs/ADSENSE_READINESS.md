## Product safeguards and release evidence
- Original, useful guides and functional tools with substantive explanations and reviewed examples.
- Manual editorial review of generated drafts; no scraped/copied articles or scaled filler pages.
- Honest publisher identity, real contact route and corrections process; do not fabricate author expertise.
- Clear navigation; no broken, empty or under-construction public sections.
- Accurate privacy disclosures matching actual storage, analytics, hosting and advertising behavior.
- Google-certified CMP integrated with TCF where required for EEA/UK/Switzerland ad serving; generic cookie banners are not a substitute.
- Ads disabled by default. Restrict inventory to reviewed, substantive public content; no ads on auth, error, private, or empty screens.
- Ads distinguishable from tools/navigation, no accidental-click encouragement, no self-clicking or incentivized/invalid traffic.
- Publisher content remains the focus and exceeds ads/promotional material.
- Owner verifies account eligibility, including Google's age requirement, ownership and domain access.
- Genuine publisher identifiers and ads.txt configuration only when supplied through proper account setup; never fabricate approval or IDs.
- Live HTTPS, crawler access, indexing controls, mobile UX and policy checks recorded before application.
